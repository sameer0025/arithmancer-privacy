Arithmancer: Math Magic: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · App: Arithmancer: Math Magic (Android, package com.sameer0025.arithmancer) · Developer: Sameer

In short: Arithmancer does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no internet access. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data.

The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of game data locally on your device: which trials you have completed, the stars you earned, which Grimoire pages you have seen, difficulty level, and your sound, music and timer settings. This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to any account. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it permanently. You can also erase it inside the game with "Erase progress" on the title screen.

Advertising, purchases and third parties

The app shows no advertising and offers no in-app purchases. It contains no analytics, advertising, social media or tracking services, and it does not share data with third parties.

Children's privacy

Arithmancer is designed for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, it does not collect personal information from children as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or similar laws. No sign-up or parental consent is needed to play.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps from Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. That is separate from this app and outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
